# Surface Brief — `index.html`

- **Scope & Mode:** Whole primary application surface · `operate`
- **Audience & Scene:** Tamil Nadu citizens standing on neighborhood streets in bright tropical daylight or evening streetlamp glare on budget Android phones or laptops, dictating code-mixed Tanglish/Tamil/English civic complaints via Wispr Flow.
- **Core Task:** Speak a complaint in one breath → resolve spoken landmark to exact `District / Zone / Ward / Depot / GIS Street ID` → stamp `<1 MB` photo evidence → file via 1-Tap Official WhatsApp / Helpline / Tamil Audio Cue (Zero-Copy-Paste) OR copy exact DOM-matched fields into TN's 6 civic portals (`GCC PGRS`, `CMWSSB`, `TWAD PUNAL`, `TANGEDCO CCMS`, `Mudhalvarin Mugavari 1100`, `TNPDS`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A daylight-first Tamil Nadu civic dispatch instrument modeled on tactile Southern Railway / Chennai Metro wayfinding boards and warm archival ledger sheets—refusing every bureaucratic grey Bootstrap wizard, hexagonal government splash page, and generic SaaS card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Sunlit limestone paper (`#F6F3EC`) and deep Palmyra ink (`#141917`) paired with high-contrast Chola terracotta vermilion (`#C83E1D`) reserved strictly for primary voice/action triggers, Kaveri teal (`#0B6E61`) for verified GIS jurisdiction locks, and warm turmeric amber (`#B86B00`) for missing-slot voice prompts; tabular numerals, crisp 1px structural rules (`#D8D1C2`), and tactile physical-button depth.

STORY: The citizen speaks naturally in Tanglish for 10 seconds, hears and sees their neighborhood landmark snap into an official Ward, Depot, and GIS Street ID, marks the street hazard on a `<1 MB` stamped photo, and dispatches the complaint in one tap via Official WhatsApp/Helpline or per-field DOM copy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top Master Bar with bilingual identity (`நம்ம குரல் · NammaKural`), 2G/Offline telemetry badge, and task mode switcher (`New Civic Grievance` vs `1100 Premature-Closure Escalation`); below, a 12-column Master-Mid-Inner workbench where the left 5 columns hold the Wispr Flow Voice Intake Dock, 5 Tanglish voice chips, live Landmark Disambiguator, and Interactive Canvas Photo Stamper, and the right 7 columns hold the Resolved GIS Jurisdiction Strip, Zero-Copy-Paste Inclusive Action Bar (Tamil TTS Read-Back + 1-Tap Official WhatsApp + Helpline Cue), and Split Portal Schema Copy Sheets.
