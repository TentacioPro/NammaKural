> **Idea: NammaKural — Tanglish-First Civic Grievance Router, Ward Resolver & Form Bridge for Tamil Nadu**
>
> **The Problem:**
> Filing a civic complaint in Tamil Nadu means navigating over nine separate portals—including Mudhalvarin Mugavari (`1100`), Greater Chennai Corporation's PGRS (`1913`), CMWSSB Metro Water (`14416`), TWAD PUNAL (`1916`), TANGEDCO CCMS (`1912`), TNPDS, and TN e-Sevai. Every portal forces citizens through clunky, English-heavy cascading dropdowns (`District → Civic Body → Zone I–XV → Ward → Department → Sub-Category`). Most residents don't know their exact Zone, Ward number, Taluk, or which agency owns an issue—confusing GCC Electrical vs. TANGEDCO for street poles, or GCC Storm Water Drains vs. CMWSSB for flooding. Even tech-literate citizens spend hours hunting ward maps and pasting text across broken forms, only for tickets to bounce later as "wrong department" or close prematurely without real fixes.
>
> **Who Has It:**
> Everyday Tamil Nadu residents, senior citizens, tenants, and working professionals who naturally describe neighborhood issues and local landmarks in spoken Tanglish or Tamil, but get blocked by fragmented portals and bureaucratic location/department menus.
>
> **Why Voice Solves It:**
> Speaking is 4× faster than typing and eliminates both department and ward guesswork. Via Wispr Flow voice input, a citizen speaks naturally for 10 seconds in Tanglish: *"Anna Nagar Tower Park back side 3rd cross-la streetlight 5 naala eriyala, transformer wire vera loose-aa irukku."* NammaKural resolves the spoken area or asks one quick nearby-landmark follow-up to auto-fill exact **District, Zone, and Ward**, splits multi-agency complaints to the right TN portals (GCC Electrical + TANGEDCO), outputs clean field-by-field English and Tamil copy-paste chunks, and drafts CM Helpline (`1100`) reopen/escalation notices if a ticket is falsely closed.
