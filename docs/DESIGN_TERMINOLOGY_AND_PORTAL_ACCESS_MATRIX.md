| Term | What to Look For | Apply It Here in `NammaKural` |

| **2. Art direction** | The particular image, crop, scale, and moment chosen to prove the mechanism | In the Evidence Photo Stamper, show the street pole hazard with the red target ring **and** the burned-in GIS footer (`ZONE 08 · WARD 103 · ROAD ID 76370 · <1MB JPEG`) at full legible scale—not a generic upload icon. |

| **4. Read-only GIS cells (`Ward 103 · Depot 103`) sit on dark slate without button chrome so nobody mistakes them for buttons. |
| **5. Progressive disclosure and information density** | When a citizen gains enough context to need the next detail, and how much they absorb at once | Show the Tamil Audio Read-Back and 1-Tap WhatsApp/Helpline buttons immediately for elders. Below that, show the clean field values for web filers, with raw DOM IDs (`#specificComplaintSubType`) tucked neatly beneath the label. |
| **6. Gestalt proximity, common region and figure–ground** | How spacing, boundaries, and background group related actions | Group the Landmark Disambiguator pills and the Traffic-Noise Slot Appender inside one tinted region (`.disambiguator-box`) because both resolve incomplete voice intake before portal splitting. |
| **7. Measure, leading and typographic rhythm** | Line length, vertical clearance for Tamil script, and repeated spacing | Keep bilingual complaint descriptions within `65–72ch` with `1.55` line-height so Tamil ascenders/descenders (*உ, ஊ, ழூ, ை*) never collide across lines. |
| **8. Alignment axis and optical alignment** | A repeated edge and how it appears to the eye | Align the left edge of every field label, the left edge of every copyable value box, and the right edge of every `Copy` button across all stacked portal cards. |
| **9. Responsive composition and source order** | How relationships survive a change from `1440px` desktop to `390px` mobile and screen readers | In DOM source order: `Voice Intake → Landmark Disambiguator → Photo Stamper → Resolved GIS Jurisdiction → Zero-Copy Audio/WhatsApp Bar → Split Portal Field Rows`. | **10. Wayfinding, deep linking and provenance** | Where am I, which official portal owns this, and where did the ward data come from? | Every resolved location names its provenance (`ArcGIS FeatureServer · EDPMobile2025 Verified · Road ID 76370`), and every portal card carries the exact live deep link (`https://gccservices.in/pgr/registercomplaints`) and official helpline. |
